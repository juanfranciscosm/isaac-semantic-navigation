SEMANTIC NAVIGATION BENCHMARK - CLIPSeg PROMPTS
================================================

Purpose:
This document records the exact CLIPSeg text prompts and inference
parameters used during the semantic navigation benchmark.

The prompt and CLIPSeg parameters remain fixed across all experimental
conditions for each object:
I0, I25, I50, O20, O50, O80.


------------------------------------------------------------
1. CARGO VEHICLE / FORKLIFTS
------------------------------------------------------------

object_name:
cargo_vehicle

CLIPSeg prompt:
yellow forklift

threshold:
0.50

max_rate_hz:
2.0

input_topic:
/rgb

Valid physical instances:
- Forklift
- Forklift_01

Evaluation:
Both forklift instances are considered valid members of the requested
semantic object class. The expected target instance depends on the
initial robot position.


------------------------------------------------------------
2. BOXES
------------------------------------------------------------

object_name:
boxes

CLIPSeg prompt:
large stack of pink plastic crates on the bottom shelf

threshold:
0.50

max_rate_hz:
2.0

input_topic:
/rgb

Ground-truth target:
BottomShelfPinkCrates

Description:
The prompt intentionally specifies the stack of pink plastic crates
located on the bottom shelf so that the requested semantic target is
distinguished from other boxes or containers present in the warehouse.

------------------------------------------------------------
FIXED BENCHMARK RULE
------------------------------------------------------------

For each object, the corresponding prompt, threshold, and maximum
inference frequency are kept unchanged across all six experimental
conditions.

Only the experimental condition changes:
- I0: nominal illumination
- I25: 25 percent reduction in illumination
- I50: 50 percent reduction in illumination
- O20: nominal geometric 20 percent bottom-up occlusion
- O50: nominal geometric 50 percent bottom-up occlusion
- O80: nominal geometric 80 percent bottom-up occlusion
